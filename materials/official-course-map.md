# learn-ai-consulting-with-phoebe - official course map

Built 2026-08-11 from verified sources (course-taking loop paused - built direct).
Bucket: aidm, difficulty 3, two-track: 6 leader (a1-a6, partners/practice leads) + 10 builder
(b1-b10, "Consultant" track). Running case: **Northbridge Advisory** (boutique firm) on a 6-week
go-to-market engagement for **Cadence, the AI note-taker** - the aidm series through-line product
(branded in ai-branding, marketed in ai-marketing, led in ai-leadership, content-engined in
ai-content) now becomes the client.

## Positioning vs siblings

- **learn-ai-research** (aidm d3): deep grounded-research techniques live THERE - b3 teaches the
  consulting-specific discipline and points for depth.
- **learn-ai-law** (aidm d2): the fake-case trap sibling; our liability rail (Deloitte Australia)
  is the consulting cousin - cross-referenced, not re-taught.
- **learn-ai-pm / learn-ai-project-management**: product/project management; engagement management
  here is consulting-shaped (client, readout, pyramid), boundary stated on b1.
- **learn-strategic-thinking** (lead d1): frameworks for the thinking itself; this course is about
  the AI-assisted PRODUCTION of consulting work.

- Financial modeling with AI (-> learn-ai-finance)
- Certificates/assessments stay with official providers

## Simulator canon (consult-live.js - ALL verified live in-browser 2026-08-11 BEFORE fan-out)

Widget: `<div id="consult-live" data-mode="MODE"></div>` + `<script src="../assets/consult-live.js?v=1">`.
Scores REAL text with real measures: answer-first opening (20), quantified support (20), so-what
density (15), weasel-word discipline (15), client anchors (15), length discipline (15).
Confidentiality gate (client names, person names, unreleased contract values, board material,
emails) caps ANY score at 15. Drafts scripted; scoring always computed live from displayed text.

| Mode (page) | Canon |
|---|---|
| storyline (b5) | ladder 19 (intern draft) -> 38 (answer first) -> 78 (quantify) -> 93 (so-whats) -> 100 (client anchors) |
| deck (b6) | "✨ Let AI write it all" = 8 (anchors 0/15, quant 0/20, fluent slop) vs "🤝 AI drafts, consultant owns" = 100 |
| confidential (b8) | leaky draft capped 15 (87 on prose alone - alarm lists the leaks) -> anonymized 87, gate open |
| capstone (b10) | presses 19 / 78 / 100 + score-your-own paste box (coaching reads at <50 / 50-79 / 80+; leak scan runs on pasted text) |

Case facts the scorer anchors on: Cadence enterprise tier $30/seat Q1; 210 pilot accounts in
procurement/security review; 62% of trial teams hit the 5-seat cap in 3 weeks; retention 94%
enterprise vs 71% individual; competitors $38-45/seat; ARPU lift 2.1x. Forbidden tokens (the
gate): "Meridian Bank", "Elena Voss", "$4.2M", "Q3 board deck", any email address.
Honesty rail on every mode: drafts are scripted rewrites, measures are real pattern checks,
nothing pasted leaves the browser.
