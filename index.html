Learn AI Consulting with Phoebe

The engagement, AI-assisted - with the rails on

The largest field experiment on consultants found AI makes them measurably faster and better - and, on tasks beyond its capability, measurably more wrong, with full confidence. This course teaches both halves. A leader track on what AI does to the pyramid, the pricing, and the firm. A consultant track that runs one six-week engagement end to end - proposal, research, analysis, storyline, deck, readout - for Cadence, the AI note-taker this series has already branded and marketed. The signature lab scores your actual executive summary against the six measures of consulting craft, and its confidentiality gate does not care how good your prose is.

The two rails

Samsung's leak and Deloitte Australia's refund, turned into policy: consumer vs enterprise tools as contract architecture, anonymization as habit, and a human owner for every fact that reaches a client.

The firm's AI operating model

Build, buy, or wrap: McKinsey's Lilli as the reference case, BCG's internal tools as the signal, and why the firm's proprietary knowledge - not its prompts - is the moat AI actually sharpens.
