# learn ai consulting with phoebe

The engagement, AI-assisted - with the rails on. Two tracks: 6 leader sessions on what AI does
to the consulting business, and 10 consultant sessions running one engagement end to end.

**Live site:** https://phoebefu6.github.io/learn-ai-consulting-with-phoebe/

## The course

Evidence-first: the Harvard/BCG jagged-frontier field experiment (758 consultants: +40% quality
inside AI's capability, 19 percentage points more wrong outside it) anchors the whole course,
alongside the Samsung leak, the Deloitte Australia refund, and the vendors' own data-handling
terms.

**Leader track** (partners, practice leads): the jagged frontier · the engagement re-priced ·
the two rails as policy · quality at AI speed · the firm's AI operating model · leading the
transition.

**Consultant track** (one six-week engagement for Cadence, the AI note-taker): meet the
engagement · proposal + scoping · research that holds up · analysis co-pilot · the storyline ·
the deck · client communications · the confidentiality drill · QA + readout · capstone.

The signature lab (`assets/consult-live.js`) scores REAL text with six real measures -
answer-first opening, quantified support, so-what density, weasel words, client anchors, length
discipline. The ladder runs 19 to 100; the "let AI write it all" anti-lever scores 8; the
confidentiality gate caps any draft at 15 the moment it names names - and the capstone's paste
box grades your own summary, in your browser only. Sources and coverage documented in
`materials/official-course-map.md`.
